Yvonne Zhang

Yvonne Zhang is a product designer and part-time filmmaker. She loves traveling and learning languages.

For the past six years, I've designed software products that have helped businesses grow and shaped product visions across B2B and B2C SaaS apps, fintech apps, e-commerce, and marketing websites.

With a cinematic eye and multi-disciplinary background, I draw connections between different industries. I specialize in end-to-end product design, where I create experiences that are intuitive, inclusive, and deeply human.

When I'm not designing, I explore the world by learning new languages, drawing, and traveling around the world.

Based in NYC. Previously @Toast, JOOR, Bizwise, and Candidly.
